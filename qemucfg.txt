Base configuration

-M pc -cpu qemu64,+rdrand -boot d -smp 2
-m 1G -serial stdio -accel tcg
-device isa-debug-exit,iobase=0xf4,iosize=0x04
-drive id=disk,file=drive.img,format=raw,if=none
-netdev user,id=net0
-monitor unix:/tmp/qemu-monitor.sock,server=on,wait=off
-qmp unix:/tmp/qemu-qmp.sock,server=on,wait=off
-device piix3-usb-uhci,id=uhci
-device virtio-keyboard-pci -device virtio-tablet-pci

Supported devices

Networking
E1000: -device e1000,netdev=net0
VirtIO: -device virtio-net-pci,netdev=net0

USB Devices
Keyboard: -device usb-kbd,bus=uhci.0,port=1
Mouse: -device usb-mouse,bus=uhci.0,port=2

RNG Devices: 
RDRAND: (switch cpu flag from base) -cpu qemu64,+rdrand
VirtIO (SLOWWWW): -device virtio-rng-pci

Block devices:
VirtIO: -device virtio-blk-pci,drive=disk
IDE (ATA): -device ide-hd,drive=disk,bus=ide.0,unit=0
AHCI: -device ich9-ahci,drive=disk
USBMSD: -device usb-storage,drive=disk
